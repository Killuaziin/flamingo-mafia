Flamingo Mafia

Flamingo Mafia is a fan page that shares short GTA 6 gameplay clips on TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram and Facebook. Every clip credits the original creator, both on screen and in the post.

Flamingo Clips Uploader

Flamingo Clips Uploader is our own internal tool. It edits our clips and publishes them only to our own accounts through the official APIs of each platform. It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account.

Contact

Instagram: @flamingomafia.br (send us a direct message). Creators who want a clip removed: message us and we remove it the same day.
