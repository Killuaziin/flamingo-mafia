Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Summary

Flamingo Clips Uploader ("the app") is an internal tool of Flamingo Mafia. It is used only by our team to publish our own videos to our own social media accounts. It does not collect data from the public.

2. What the app accesses

When we connect one of our own accounts (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or Facebook), the platform gives the app an access token with the permissions we approve, such as basic profile information (account name and ID) and permission to upload and publish videos. The app uses this only to publish our clips and to confirm which account it is connected to.

3. What we store

Access tokens are stored only on our own computer, in a private folder, and are never shared, sold or sent to third parties. We do not store data about viewers or other users, and we do not use cookies or analytics on this website.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell or share personal data. Videos are sent only to the platform where they are being published.

5. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in each platform's settings (for example, TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions; Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions). When access is revoked, the app can no longer publish to that account, and we delete the stored token.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children and is used only by our adult team members.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.

8. Contact

Instagram direct message: @flamingomafia.br.
